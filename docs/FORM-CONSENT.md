# Form and Issue Consent

**Last updated:** 2026-09-21

## Current status

The current repository, browser extension, and test fixture do not contain a project-operated contact form, newsletter form, account form, payment form, or feedback form. No form-consent banner is therefore implemented in the current code.

GitHub Issues, Discussions, pull requests, and security reports are operated by GitHub. Information submitted there is processed under GitHub's terms and privacy policy, not under a project-operated form system.

## Future form requirement

Before adding a project-operated form, the implementation should display a clear notice at the point of collection stating the purpose of collection, the categories of information requested, the recipient or service provider, the retention period or deletion method, and the user's available choices. A non-essential marketing or analytics opt-in should be separate from a necessary submission action, should not be preselected, and should be recorded in a way that supports withdrawal.

Do not collect passwords, payment credentials, authentication tokens, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through a future form. This template is not legal advice and should be reviewed for the jurisdictions and providers involved before use.
